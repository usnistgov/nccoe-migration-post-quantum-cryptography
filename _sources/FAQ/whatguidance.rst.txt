Has NIST published strategy to manage and guide the transition to post-quantum cryptography?
********************************************************************************************

`NIST IR 8547 \(FINAL\) Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2026/NIST.IR.8547.pdf>`_ 
This report identifies existing quantum-vulnerable cryptographic standards and the quantum-resistant standards to which information technology products and services will need to transition. 

This report serves as the initial step in a broader strategy to manage and guide the transition to post-quantum cryptography. It provides the technical framework necessary to meet the accelerated migration timelines for federal high value assets and high impact systems while serving as a blueprint for critical infrastructure owners and operators and other organizations undergoing the same shift. This transition will involve the adoption of new PQC algorithms as well as the careful deprecation and eventual removal of quantum-vulnerable algorithms that are currently widespread in technological infrastructures. Public-private engagement will be crucial on the path toward PQC.
