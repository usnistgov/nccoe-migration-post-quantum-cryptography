Are there mappings for aspects of Migration to PQC?
***************************************************

If your organization describes its maturity and mitigations to cybersecurity and privacy risk via frameworks like the NIST CSF or Security and Privacy Controls, a mapping to aspects of Migration to PQC could help support commitments to address the threat of a cryptographically relelvant quantum computer.

*   Deloitte, `Cryptographic Resilience: A Cyber Security Framework \(CSF\) 2.0 Community Profile <https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/services/consulting/2026/deloitte-cryptographic-resilience-community.pdf>`_

*   `NIST CSWP 48, Mappings of Migration to PQC Project Capabilities to Risk Framework <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.48.ipd.pdf>`_
