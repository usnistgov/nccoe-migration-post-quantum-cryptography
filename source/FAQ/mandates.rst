Is there a US mandate for action related to CRQC-readiness or migration to PQC?
*******************************************************************************

The most US Government call for Federal Execution of the Migration to PQC is in the `Office of Management and Budget Memorandum M-26-15  <https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/M-26-15-Execution-of-the-Migration-to-Post-Quantum-Cryptography.pdf>`_.
