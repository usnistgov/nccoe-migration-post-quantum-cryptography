What is "cryptographic debt" and why does it matter?
***************************************************************************************


Cryptographic debt is the accumulated risk that builds up when cryptography is deployed without a centralized strategy, scattered across dozens of applications and never inventoried.
 The result is that deprecated or insecure algorithms can lurk in systems for years undetected. See QuSecure's `Tackling Today's Unrealized Cryptographic Debt Challenge <https://www.qusecure.com/tackling-todays-unrealized-cryptographic-debt-challenge/>`_ and the `Orchestrated Crypto-Agility Whitepaper <https://www.qusecure.com/resources/orchestrated-crypto-agility/>`_
