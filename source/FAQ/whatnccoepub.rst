What has the National Cybersecurity Center of Excellence \(NCCoE\) published to support migration to PQC?
*********************************************************************************************************

The following preliminary draft were published before 2024 and frame the challenges that the project could accomplish:
`Preparation for Considering the Implementation and Adoption of Quantum Safe Cryptography <https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-04/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38a-preliminary-draft.pdf>`_
`Quantum Readiness: Cryptographic Discovery <https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38b-preliminary-draft.pdf>`_
`Quantum Readiness: Testing Draft Standards <https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/pqc-migration-nist-sp-1800-38c-preliminary-draft.pdf>`_

The are no current plans to issue second drafts or final versions of these publications.

The project team is using NIST Cybersecurity White Papers to share progress.

The following are two NIST Cybersecurity White Papers published:
`NIST CSWP 48, Mappings of Migration to PQC Project Capabilities to Risk Framework <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.48.ipd.pdf>`_
`NIST CSWP 39, Considerations for Achieving Crypto Agility Strategies and Practices <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.39.pdf>`_
