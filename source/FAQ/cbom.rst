What is a Cryptography Bill of Materials (CBOM) and how do I use it for Post-Quantum Systems and Applications?
*****************************************************************************************************

A CBOM is a list of all the cryptographic algorithms, libraries, and protocols used in a system or application. It provides transparency into the cryptographic components and their configurations, which is essential for assessing security risks and planning migrations to post-quantum cryptography (PQC).

There are a few resources from different organizations that can help you understand and utilize CBOMs:

*   CycloneDX, `Authoritative Guide to CBOM <https://cyclonedx.org/guides/OWASP_CycloneDX-Authoritative-Guide-to-CBOM-en.pdf>`_.

*   QuSecure, `Cryptographic bill of Materials (CBOM): Continuous Cryptographic Visibility for Crypto-Agility and Post-Quantum Readiness <https://www.qusecure.com/cryptographic-bill-of-materials-cbom/>`_.
