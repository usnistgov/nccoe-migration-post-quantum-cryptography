Are there performance results posted for PQC algorithms?
********************************************************

* Each submitted algorithm such as `Crystals Kyber <https://pq-crystals.org/kyber/index.shtml/>`_ includes a "Performance Overview Section"

* Papers have been submitted to the NIST PQC Standardization Conferences such as `(Yet another) Analysis of MLWE’s performance impact on specific TLS Use-cases <https://csrc.nist.gov/csrc/media/events/2025/sixth-pqc-standardization-conference/(yet%20another)%20analysis%20of%20mlwes%20performance%20impact.pdf>`_ .

* Open Quantum Safe, `OQS algorithm performance visualizations <https://openquantumsafe.org/benchmarking/>`_
